Room for Duo

Wide and desktop web on iPhone Duo

A free Safari extension for iPhone Duo. Folded, websites use the whole outer display, with text and buttons clear of Safari's controls and the camera. Unfold it, and sites switch to their desktop versions automatically.

No ads, no subscription, no data collected.
