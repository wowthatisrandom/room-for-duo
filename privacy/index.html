Privacy Policy

Room for Duo · Updated November 24, 2026

Room for Duo collects no data and makes no network requests.

  • Your layout choices for each website are stored in Safari on your device.
  • When Apple Intelligence is on, Room for Duo sends short descriptions of page elements (tag names, class names, a few words of text, sizes) to Apple's on-device model through the Foundation Models framework. This happens on your iPhone; nothing is sent to HigherBar Apps or anyone else. The model's answers are stored in Safari on your device.
  • In desktop mode on the open screen, Room for Duo changes the User-Agent that Safari sends to websites to Safari's Mac version, so sites serve their desktop pages. It doesn't read or keep what you browse.
  • No analytics, no accounts, no tracking, no ads.

Safari asks you to allow Room for Duo on websites because it changes how pages are laid out. That access is used only on your device to adjust pages.
